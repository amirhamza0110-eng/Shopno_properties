About the company

Welcome to Shopno Properties, your trusted partner in the world of real estate. We are a team of passionate professionals committed to providing honest, transparent, and personalized real estate solutions to help our clients buy, sell, and invest with confidence.

Based in Gulshan, Dhaka, Shopno Properties has built a reputation for integrity, innovation, and customer satisfaction, offering high-quality properties and expert consultancy across Bangladesh’s thriving real estate market.

Who We Are

At Shopno Properties, we believe real estate isn’t just about land and buildings — it’s about dreams, security, and opportunities. Our experienced team specializes in residential, commercial, and investment properties, ensuring every client finds the perfect match for their needs.

We focus on creating value-driven developments that combine modern architecture, premium amenities, and strategic locations. Every project we handle reflects our dedication to excellence, reliability, and long-term growth.

Our Mission

To deliver trustworthy and innovative real estate solutions that empower individuals and investors to make confident property decisions. We strive to make real estate transactions simple, transparent, and beneficial for all parties — helping our clients achieve their dreams through smart investments.

Our Vision

To become one of Bangladesh’s leading real estate companies, recognized for our trust, quality developments, and client-first service. We envision a future where Shopno Properties stands as a symbol of integrity and innovation in the property market of Dhaka and beyond.

Why Choose Shopno Properties?

At Shopno Properties, your goals are our priority. Whether you’re buying your first home or expanding your investment portfolio, we provide end-to-end real estate support to make your journey easy and rewarding.

  • Experienced and professional real estate team
  • Transparent transactions with full legal and financial clarity
  • High-quality projects in prime locations
  • Personalized client service from start to finish
  • Trusted by hundreds of satisfied clients across Bangladesh
